What To Do Next

This page turns the strategy into a presentation plan and a next-analysis plan so your team can move from polished dashboard to final recommendation deck.

1

Lead with the strongest story

Open with follow-up gaps after acute or fracture-heavy journeys.

  • Use the gap distribution chart.
  • Frame the outcome as reduced delayed care.
  • Tie it to scheduling or outreach.
2

Add a second strategic lane

Use high-utilizer segmentation as a system-efficiency and coordination story.

  • Show how the mean exceeds the median.
  • Distinguish chronic complexity from fragmented repeat use.
  • Recommend segmented interventions.
3

Use SDOH carefully

Keep it as a supporting overlay among screened patients.

  • Remove unspecified responses.
  • Focus on transportation, food, and financial strain.
  • Avoid population-wide claims.
Best Next Analyses

High-value extensions

  • Build a ranked table of median days to next encounter by diagnosis group.
  • Create department transition paths such as ED to imaging to orthopedics.
  • Construct patient-level utilization segments and compare their department footprints.
  • Add confidence intervals or simple statistical checks for the biggest subgroup findings.
